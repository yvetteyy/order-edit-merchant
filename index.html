Shopify fulfillment hold
Turn on if your downstream system imports orders by Shopify fulfillment status.
Sets Shopify fulfillment to on hold during the editing window, then releases it when the window closes ×
Auto payment capture
Turn on if your downstream system imports orders by payment status, and your Shopify payment has changed to "Manual"
Keeps payment status as "Authorized" during the editing window, then captures it to paid when window closes. ×

Add block

Block visibility
Step 1: Install AfterShip Order Edits block
Go to editor for adding your app block
Open the Shopify checkout editor, find AfterShip Order Edits in the block list, and add it to your Thank you page and Order status page.
Save and come back here
Click "Save" in the editor, then return here. The block won't go live yet until you set its visibility in the last step.
Step 2: Test the block with a test order
Test order edits via an test order. Didn't sync the edited order to your Shipping platform or ERP? Learn more
